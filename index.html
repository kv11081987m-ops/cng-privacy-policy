CNG Near Me — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 11 November 2026

CNG Near Me (“the app”) helps drivers find CNG pumps and see whether CNG is available. It is made by SetuSphere (“we”, “us”). This policy explains what information the app uses, why, and the choices you have. If you have any question, write to us at setusphere@gmail.com.

In short: we have no ads and no advertising ID. We do not sell or share your personal information. Location is used only while the app is open — never in the background. Your name, mobile number and email are optional and are collected only if you contact us; we use them only to reply to you.

2. Location in detail

  • We ask for location permission while you use the app (foreground). The app never asks for or uses background location.
  • Near-pump reminder: while the app is open, it checks your position on your phone. If you stay within about 100 metres of a CNG pump for about a minute (and are not travelling fast), the app shows a popup asking if you want to update the status. This check happens on your device; your live position is not stored or sent to us for this reminder. The reminder appears at most once per pump every 2 hours.
  • When you choose “Use my current location” (suggesting a pump) or “Fix Location” (adding a missing pump location), the coordinates you confirm are sent to us and saved with that suggestion or pump.
  • You can turn location off at any time in your phone’s settings; you can still search pumps by city.

3. How we use information

  • To show CNG pumps and their latest status to all drivers.
  • To review pump suggestions, locations and reports, and to prevent misuse.
  • To reply to your contact requests.
  • To keep the service secure and working.

We do not use your information for advertising, marketing, or profiling, and we do not sell it.

4. Who can see your information

  • Other users see a pump’s latest status (available / not available, pressure, queue) and how long ago it was updated. They do not see who reported it.
  • Our administrators can see suggestions (including photos), reports and contact requests so they can review and reply.
  • Service providers: we use Supabase to store our database and files. Our data is stored on servers in Mumbai, India (AWS ap-south-1) and is sent over encrypted (HTTPS) connections. Supabase acts on our behalf and does not use it for its own purposes.
  • Google Maps: the map is displayed using the Google Maps SDK. Google may receive technical information such as your IP address and device details when map tiles are loaded, as described in Google’s Privacy Policy. Opening “Get Directions” hands you over to the Google Maps app or website.
  • We do not share your personal information with anyone else, except if the law requires us to.

5. How long we keep information

  • Contact requests (including name, mobile number and email): kept for 12 months after the request is resolved, then deleted.
  • Pump status reports and device ID records: kept as long as needed to show current pump information and to prevent abuse.
  • Pump suggestions and photos: kept while needed to review and maintain the pump data.

6. Your choices and rights

  • Location, photos, and contact details are optional. You can use the main features without giving your name, mobile number or email.
  • You can ask us to access, correct or delete the personal information we hold about you (for example your contact request) by emailing setusphere@gmail.com from the email address you used, or quoting the mobile number you gave. We will respond within a reasonable time.
  • Uninstalling the app removes the random device ID from your phone. Reports already sent remain, but they cannot be linked to you personally.
  • If you are a pump owner and a pump’s information is wrong, use “Was this info incorrect?” in the app or email us.

7. Security

Data is sent over HTTPS. Access to the database is restricted: the app can only use specific, controlled functions, and only our administrators can read suggestions, photos and contact requests. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure, but we take reasonable steps to protect your information.

8. Children

The app is meant for drivers and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

9. Third-party data

Some pump locations are based on data from © Overture Maps Foundation and, where stated, OpenStreetMap contributors. Pump information may not always be accurate; please verify before you travel.

10. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy we will update the date at the top. If a change is significant we will also tell you inside the app.
