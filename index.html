CNG Near Me by CNGSetu · SetuSphere

Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 30, 2026

Our commitment: CNG Near Me is a crowdsourced public-good app. There is no login, and we do not ask for your name, phone number or email address. Contributions are tied only to a random device identifier, not to who you are.

1. About This App

CNG Near Me ("the App") helps drivers in India find nearby CNG fuel stations and check real-time availability. The App is operated by SetuSphere, Deoria, Uttar Pradesh, India (MSME Registration: UDYAM-UP-21-0060784).

The App operates on a crowdsourced model. Users voluntarily report CNG pump status (available / unavailable, pressure, queue length) and can suggest new pumps, to help other drivers. No account creation or login is required.

3. Location Data

The App requests access to your device's GPS location only while the App is open and in use (foreground access). We do not access your location in the background.

  • Your location is used to center the map and calculate distance to nearby CNG pumps. For this use, your coordinates are not stored on our servers and not shared with third parties.
  • Pump suggestions: if you use "Use My Location" while suggesting a pump, the coordinates are stored as the pump's location.
  • Fix Location: if you use "Fix Location" on a pump that has no location, your coordinates at that moment are saved as that pump's location, and are recorded together with your device ID in a log that only our team can see.
  • In both cases the coordinates describe the pump, not your home or any personal address.
  • You may deny location permission. The App will still work and you can search for a city manually.

4. Anonymous Device ID

To maintain data quality, the App generates a random identifier (UUID) the first time you use it. This ID is:

  • Stored on your device using local storage
  • Not linked to your name, phone, account, or any personal information
  • Used to enforce a 2-hour cooldown on status reports per pump, to prevent spam, to show you your own pump suggestions and their review result, to count your reward points and badge, and to decide whether you can use features such as "Fix Location"
  • Removed from your device if you uninstall the App or clear its data. Records already saved on our servers stay until they are deleted as described in Section 8.

5. Crowdsourced Reports

When you submit a pump status update (CNG availability, pressure, queue), the following is stored in our database:

  • The pump identifier (which station you reported about)
  • The report content (available / not available, pressure rating, queue length)
  • A timestamp
  • Your anonymous device ID (as described above)

These records are not attached to any personal identity. The consensus status displayed to users is computed from the last 2 hours of reports, weighted by contributor trust level.

6. Pump Suggestions, Photos and Rewards

If you use the "Suggest a Pump" feature, you may voluntarily submit:

  • Pump name, address, city and state (required)
  • Operating hours, operator and notes (optional)
  • GPS coordinates of the pump location, if you use the "Use My Location" button (optional)
  • A photo of the pump's name board (optional)

This information is stored in our database and reviewed by our team before the pump is added to the pump directory. Your device ID is attached to the suggestion so that we can prevent spam and show you, on the "My Suggestions" screen, whether your suggestion is pending, approved or not approved (with a reason, if we give one). When a suggestion is approved, you may be shown a thank-you message and awarded reward points and a badge.

Photos. The App asks for camera or gallery access only when you tap the button to add a board photo, and works fully without it. The photo is resized and compressed before it is sent, and is kept in a private storage area that only our reviewers can open. It is not shown in the App, not shown to other users, and not copied to the public pump directory. Please photograph only the pump's name board and avoid people and vehicle number plates.

Approved suggestions become pump directory entries without your device ID and without the photo.

7. Third-Party Services

  • Google Maps (Android): The App uses Google Maps SDK to display the map. Google's own Privacy Policy applies to map rendering. We do not share your location data with Google beyond what the map SDK requires for display.
  • Supabase: Our backend database and file storage are hosted on Supabase (supabase.com). Pump data, device IDs, suggestions, reward points and uploaded pump board photos are stored on Supabase servers. Supabase's Privacy Policy applies.

We do not use advertising SDKs, analytics trackers, crash reporters, or social login SDKs at this time.

8. Data Retention

  • Pump status reports: Reports older than 2 hours stop counting towards the displayed status. We may retain historical data for service improvement.
  • Device IDs and points: Kept in our database for as long as they are linked to reports, suggestions or points. No personal identity is linked to these IDs.
  • Pump suggestions: Kept with their review result. Approved suggestions become permanent pump directory entries (without device ID).
  • Photos: Kept for review and verification, and not published. We may delete them at any time.
  • Fix Location log: Kept to prevent abuse of the feature.

9. Children's Privacy

CNG Near Me is not directed at children under 18 years of age. We do not knowingly collect any information from children. If you believe a child has submitted information through our App, please contact us and we will delete it promptly.

10. Your Rights

Because we do not ask for your name, phone number or email, there is no personal profile to access or correct. However, you may:

  • Deny location, camera or photo permission at any time through your device settings
  • Clear the App's local data (which removes your anonymous device ID from your device) through your device's app settings
  • Contact us to request deletion of any pump suggestion, photo, or other record you submitted (if you can identify it)

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy as the App evolves. We will post the updated policy at this URL and update the "Last Updated" date above. Continued use of the App after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
